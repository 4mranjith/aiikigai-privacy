Help Center — AI Ikigai

Last updated: 3 October 2026

Getting started

Answer the four Ikigai sections (what you love, what you are good at, what the world needs, what you can be paid for). When you finish, tap "Connect the Dots" to get your personalised insight. Answers are autosaved on your device as you type.

Voice typing

Tap the microphone button in an answer box and speak. If it does nothing, allow the microphone permission in Android Settings → Apps → AI Ikigai → Permissions, and make sure your phone's speech recognition (Google) is enabled.

What is free and what is Premium?

  • Free: all eight core insights and the full PDF export.
  • Premium: extra insights — career recommendations, psychological insights and the Ikigai Sweet Spot.

Subscribing, restoring and cancelling

  • Subscribe: open any locked Premium card and choose Monthly or Yearly. Payment is handled by Google Play.
  • Premium missing after buying or reinstalling? Sign in to the app with the same account you used when you bought, then fully close and reopen the app so your subscription re-syncs. Make sure the Play Store is signed in to the account that paid.
  • Cancel: Google Play → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → AI Ikigai → Cancel. Premium stays active until the end of the period you paid for.
  • Refunds: handled by Google Play — see Google Play refund help.

Sign-in problems

Check your internet connection and update the app from Google Play. If Google sign-in fails, try again after clearing the app's cache, or sign in with email.

Insight did not generate

Insights need an internet connection. Wait a moment and try again; your answers are saved and will not be lost.

Your data

In the app: Settings → Your data → Download my data gives you your answers and latest insight as a file, and Delete account permanently removes your account, answers and insights immediately. Deleting your account does not cancel a Premium subscription — cancel it first in Google Play → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions.

Cannot open the app? Email us with the subject "Delete my AI Ikigai data" from the address on your account and we will delete it within 30 days. See also the Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.
